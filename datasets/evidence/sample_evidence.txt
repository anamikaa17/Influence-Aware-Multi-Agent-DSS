Normal network traffic generally follows expected communication patterns.

A sudden increase in traffic volume may indicate suspicious network behaviour.

Repeated failed login attempts can indicate a brute force attack.

Unexpected communication with unknown external IP addresses may indicate malicious activity.

Port scanning involves attempts to connect to multiple ports on a target system.

Denial of Service attacks may involve unusually high volumes of requests.

Anomalous packet sizes and unusual connection durations can indicate network intrusion.

Network traffic should be evaluated using multiple features rather than a single indicator.

A high traffic volume alone does not prove that an attack is occurring.

Security decisions should be supported by independent evidence.
